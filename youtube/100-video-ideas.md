# 100 video ideas — "Money & Power Stories" (Arabic, faceless)

Niche: narrative documentaries about scams, collapses, tycoons and brands, told for an Arab audience.

**How to read this list**

- Ranked by my estimate of pull for an Arab audience (Arab relevance + story tension + name recognition). It is a judgment, not measured data. Validate the top 10 by searching each title on YouTube before producing.
- Titles are working titles. Rewrite them after you see which ones competitors already used.
- Every number and date here must be re-checked against two sources while scripting. I wrote them from memory to show the angle.

**Pillars:** (A) Scams & heists · (B) Collapses · (C) Arab empires & tycoons · (D) Economy explained · (E) Brand secrets
